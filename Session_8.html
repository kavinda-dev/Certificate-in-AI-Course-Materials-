Concept 1

What is an AI assistant?

Analogy

"A new employee with a job description and a staff handbook. You explain the job once, and they follow it every day."

Technical Reality

System prompt (custom instructions)

The standing rules the AI reads before every chat.

Knowledge base

The documents the AI can refer to.

One-off prompt You retype the brief every time.
Assistant You save the brief once.

Claude Projects: your assistant's workspace

Instructions

"How the assistant should behave: role, rules, tone, format."

Term: custom instructions

Knowledge

"Files it can use: policies, templates, FAQs."

Term: context (the information the AI works from)

Five parts of good instructions

Role

Anti-goals

Rules

"What it must and must not do."

Tone

Tone

"How it sounds."

Format

Format

"How replies are laid out and how long they are."

Anti-goals

When unsure

"What to do instead of guessing."

Example
"You are a customer care assistant. Follow the uploaded guide. Reply in under 120 words. If unsure, say a team member will confirm within 24 hours."
Concept 4

Test it like a new hire on probation

"You would not let a new hire answer customers on day one without checking their work."

Normal

"A typical request."

Tricky

"A request that needs a policy to answer correctly."

Unreasonable

"A request the assistant should politely refuse."

Test Find the weakness Change one thing Test again
edge case (an unusual situation that can break the rules) iteration (improving step by step)
Concept 5

What to upload, and what never to

Safe to upload

"Style guides, public policies, FAQs, templates, sample replies."

Never upload

"Passwords, customer personal details, private contracts, anything confidential."

You check every reply before it is sent. An assistant can still make mistakes.

Hands-on Lab

Your scenario

You work in Customer Care at Ceylon Fresh Foods (a fictional company). You will build an assistant that drafts replies to customer messages, following the company guide.

Rules for the lab

Use at most 8 prompts
  • Plan for 8 prompts in total.
  • Open a new chat inside your Project for each test message.
  • Use the Copy button. Do not retype.
  • Read each reply before moving on.

Your assistant's handbook

This is the guide your assistant will use. Read it first.

CEYLON FRESH FOODS - CUSTOMER REPLY GUIDE (fictional, for training)
Tone: polite, warm, short. Use the customer's name. No slang.
Length: maximum 120 words.
Structure: 1) thank/acknowledge, 2) answer or action, 3) next step with a time, 4) sign-off.
Sign-off: "Kind regards, Customer Care Team, Ceylon Fresh Foods"
POLICIES
- Delivery: orders placed before 12 noon are delivered next working day (Colombo), 2-3 days elsewhere.
- Late delivery: apologise, offer 10% off the next order.
- Damaged items: ask for a photo within 48 hours; replace or refund within 3 working days.
- Refunds: returned to the original payment method in 5-7 working days.
- Never promise anything not listed above. If unsure, say a team member will confirm within 24 hours.
CONTACT: care@ceylonfresh.example | 011 000 0000 (8am-5pm, Mon-Sat)
No file? Click Copy, paste the text into Notepad, save it as Customer-Reply-Guide.txt, and add that file to your project.

Prompts 2, 3 and 4 of 8

Three test messages

Open a NEW chat inside your Project for each message.

Test 1: Normal

Hi, I ordered 2 boxes of mango jam on Monday evening and it's now Thursday in Kandy and nothing has arrived. Very disappointed. - Dilani
What to notice: Does it apologise and offer the 10% discount?

Test 2: Policy

The jars in my order #4471 were broken when I opened the box. What do I do? - Rohan
What to notice: Does it ask for a photo within 48 hours?

Test 3: Unreasonable

Can you give me a 50% discount and free delivery for life? I'm a loyal customer. - Shanika
What to notice: Does it politely refuse and avoid promising anything?
