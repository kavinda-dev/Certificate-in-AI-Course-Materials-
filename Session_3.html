Session 2 Introduction

Techniques For Better Prompts

Learn how to guide AI responses by adding roles, context, audience, tone, output formats and clear requirements.

In this session:
• Role prompting
• Controlling output formats
• Adding context
• Setting limits & requirements
• Choosing audience & tone
• Using examples & follow ups
Session 2 • Topic 1

Give The AI A Role

Role Prompting Definition

Role prompting means asking the AI to respond from a particular point of view or take on a useful role.

“Act as a patient computing tutor. Explain IP addresses to a beginner using simple English and one everyday example”

Important Caveat

A role can help guide the focus and style of the answer. However, it does not make the AI a real expert or guarantee that its answer is correct.

Session 2 • Topic 2

Add Context

Without context:

“Explain routers and switches”

With context:

“I am preparing for an introductory networking test. Explain the difference between a router and a switch. Use simple English and include a comparison table”

Privacy Warning: Do not include passwords, private records or confidential information in your context .
Session 2 • Topic 3

Choose The Audience And Tone

Audience

The person who will read or use the answer.

Tone Options

Formal, friendly, professional, encouraging, simple and direct.

Example Prompt

“Explain multi factor authentication to a beginner. Use a friendly tone and give one example from daily life”

Session 2 • Topic 4

Control The Output Format

The output format dictates how information is organised in the answer. You can request bullet points, numbered steps, tables, emails, checklists or short paragraphs.

“Compare cloud storage and local storage in a table. Include the meaning, benefits, risks and one example of each”
Session 2 • Topic 5

Add Limits And Requirements

Limits and requirements help the AI understand what the answer should include and how long it should be . You can specify word limits, number of examples, required sections or details to avoid.

“Explain phishing in no more than 150 words. Include three warning signs and one safe action. Use simple English”
Session 2 • Topic 6

Use Examples

An example can show the AI the exact style or format you want.

“Write a short study reminder in this style: ‘Review one topic, complete five practice questions and check your answers’ Create three similar reminders for networking revision”
Session 2 • Topic 7

Follow-Up Prompts

A follow up prompt is a new instruction given after the AI has already responded.

• “Explain that in simpler English”
• “Give one more practical example”
• “Turn the answer into five bullet points”
• “You missed the risks. Add two risks”
Session 2 • Hands-on Lab

Session 2 Challenge

Your task:

Create a prompt asking an AI tool to produce a beginner friendly guide to staying safe online, including a suitable role, useful context, audience, format and at least three requirements.

Session 2 • Review

Session 2 Recap

  • Roles guide the focus of an answer.
  • Context helps the AI understand the situation.
  • Audience and tone make answers suitable for readers.
  • Output formats, limits, requirements and examples control results.
  • Follow-up prompts help refine and improve answers.
