Session 1 • Topic 1

Welcome To Prompt Engineering

Core Concept

Prompt engineering helps us explain what we need from an AI tool. A clear prompt gives the AI more information about the task and the answer we expect.

Comparison Example

Basic prompt: “Write about cyber security”
Improved prompt: “Explain three common cyber security risks to a first-year computing student. Use simple English and give one example for each risk”

Session 1 • Topic 3

What Is A Prompt?

A prompt is a question or instruction given to an AI tool. It tells the tool what you want it to do.

Prompt A:

“Explain networks”

Prompt B:

“Explain what a computer network is to a beginner. Use simple English and give two everyday examples”

Prompt B explains the topic, the audience and the type of answer needed.

Session 1 • Topic 4

Weak Prompts And Improved Prompts

Example 1
Weak: “Write an email”
Improved: “Write a polite email to my lecturer asking for an assignment extension. Keep it under 120 words and use a respectful tone”
Example 2
Weak: “Explain AI”
Improved: “Explain Artificial Intelligence to a student who is new to computing. Use simple English and include three everyday examples”
Example 3
Weak: “Make a plan”
Improved: “Create a five-day study plan for a student who has two hours available each evening. Include short breaks and revision time”
Session 1 • Topic 5

Four Useful Parts Of A Prompt

1. Task

What do you want the AI to do?

2. Context

What background information does the AI need?

3. Audience

Who will read or use the answer?

4. Requirements

How should the answer be written or organised?

Example prompt:

“Explain phishing emails to a beginner computing student. Give three warning signs and one example. Use simple English and present the answer as bullet points”

Session 1 • Practical Lab

Effective Prompting Through Image Generation

In this activity, we will generate the same type of image using four different prompts, comparing results to see how adding clear details guides an AI image generator.

Challenge: Create an image of a futuristic city.
Level 1 • Image Generation

The Basic Prompt

“Generate an image of a futuristic city”

This prompt gives the AI very little information:

  • Setting is not specified.
  • Colours and lighting are not specified.
  • Image style is not defined.
  • Composition is left entirely to the AI.
Level 2 • Image Generation

Add Some Details

“Generate an image of a futuristic city at night with tall buildings, bright neon lights and flying cars”
Setting: At night
Buildings: Tall buildings
Lighting: Bright neon lights
Objects: Flying cars
Level 3 • Image Generation

Give Clear Visual Instructions

“Generate a futuristic city at night with tall glass skyscrapers, glowing blue and purple neon lights and flying cars between the buildings. Show a busy city street with people walking below. Use a cinematic science fiction style, realistic lighting and a wide-angle view”

Provides enhanced control over style, lighting, composition, activity and color.

Level 4 • Image Generation

The Full Prompt

“Create a high-quality, realistic science fiction image of a futuristic city at night. Show tall glass skyscrapers with detailed architecture and glowing blue and purple neon lights. Include flying cars moving between the buildings and a busy street with small groups of people walking below. Add reflections of the city lights on wet roads. Use cinematic lighting, realistic shadows, fine details and a wide-angle composition. Make the city feel advanced, lively and believable. Keep the main skyscrapers as the central focus of the image”

Includes comprehensive instructions regarding environment, architecture, lighting, style, composition and mood.

Analysis • Image Generation

Compare The Four Images

Display and evaluate the four images side by side to assess how added specifics affect output quality and alignment.

Session 1 • Summary

Lessons From Image Generation

Effective prompts guide image generation by detailing subject, setting, style, lighting and composition.

Key Takeaway: A longer prompt is not automatically better. The goal is clear, relevant details matching the intended result.

Session 1 • Final Recap

Session 1 Recap

  • A prompt is a question or instruction given to an AI tool.
  • Prompt engineering means writing and improving prompts.
  • Clear prompts lead to more useful responses.
  • Context and details help the AI understand the task.
  • Audience, format and follow-up prompts enhance final results.
