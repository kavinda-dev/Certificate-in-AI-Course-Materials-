Session 3 Introduction

Practical Prompt Engineering

Use prompt engineering for everyday practical tasks, summarising information, breaking tasks into steps and using AI responsibly.

In this session:
• Practical AI tasks
• Summarising & transforming info
• Prompt chaining / steps
• Checking responses
• Privacy & safety
• Practical challenges
Session 3 • Topic 1

Choose A Practical Task

AI Support Areas

  • • Study and revision
  • • Emails and messages
  • • Summaries and planning
  • • Generating ideas & outlines

Guidelines

AI should support your work. Always review results and follow the rules that apply to your studies or workplace.

Session 3 • Topic 2

Summarise And Transform Information

Ask the AI to change information into an easier format (e.g., turn notes into bullet points, summarize long text, convert instructions into a checklist or explain ideas in simple English).

“Turn these notes into a checklist. Keep all important tasks and use short action statements”
Session 3 • Topic 3

Break A Large Task Into Steps

Prompt chaining means using a series of prompts to complete a larger task where each step builds on the result of an earlier step.

Step 1Suggest ideas
Step 2Create outline
Step 3Add examples
Step 4Conclusion
Session 3 • Topic 4

Check The AI Response

Verification Checklist

• Facts and figures
• Missing details
• Unclear statements
• Unsupported claims
• Biased or unfair wording
• Reliable sources
Remember: A confident answer is not proof that the answer is correct.
Session 3 • Topic 5

Privacy And Responsible Use

  • Do not enter passwords or private account details.
  • Do not share confidential workplace information.
  • Avoid entering private student or customer records.
  • Follow school, college and workplace rules.
  • Be honest about AI use when required.
Session 3 • Practical Lab

Practical Challenge

Choose a study plan, polite email, summary or computing explanation, then complete the prompt-improvement cycle and fact check results.

Session 3 • Review

Final Review

  • Clear prompts, context, roles and formats guide effective AI outputs.
  • Large tasks can be split into steps via prompt chaining.
  • AI responses must always be verified for privacy and accuracy.
Session 3 • Activity

Final Interactive Activity: Write Your Own Prompt

Write one prompt you can use for your studies or daily life, ensuring the task, context, audience, format and requirements are clearly defined.
