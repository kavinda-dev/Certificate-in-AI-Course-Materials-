Session 6 • Topic 3

Responsible AI, Ethics & Guardrails

Data Privacy & PII

Never input confidential client data or passwords into public models. Always scrub personally identifiable info.

Hallucination Detection

Always cross-verify numerical stats, financial quotes, and legal citations generated by LLMs.

Human-in-the-Loop

Maintain mandatory human review and accountability for all AI-assisted workplace assets.
